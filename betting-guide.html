Betting Guide

New to the league, or just want a refresher? Here's how the bets work.

Spread

Vegas determines a favorite and an underdog for each game and sets what's called the Spread to make the bet more fair. The Spread is the point margin the favorite is supposed to win by / the underdog is supposed to lose by. If the spread is negative, the team named next to it is the favorite and is supposed to win by that many points (e.g. "Clemson -4" means they're supposed to beat the other team by 4 points). If the spread is positive, that team is supposed to lose by that many points (e.g. "LSU +4" means they're supposed to lose by 4 points, or better). By taking the spread, you believe the team you pick is going to beat that point margin.

Using "Clemson -4 vs LSU +4": if you bet Clemson -4, you believe Clemson wins by more than 4 points. If you bet LSU +4, you believe LSU loses by less than 4 points, or wins outright.

A "Push" is when the margin of victory lands exactly on the spread — nobody wins or loses, and the bet is refunded. Using the same example, if Clemson beats LSU 18–14, the margin is exactly 4, a Push. To avoid this, Vegas often uses half points, since there's no such thing as half a point in a real game. Example: "Ohio St -2.5 vs Texas +2.5." Betting Ohio St -2.5 means you believe they win by 3+ points. Betting Texas +2.5 means you believe Texas loses by 2 or fewer points, or wins outright.

Moneyline

A moneyline bet is simply which team you think wins the game outright — no point margin involved. Betting the Cowboys moneyline means you believe the Cowboys win, period.

Vegas makes moneyline bets fair by adjusting the odds: the lower (more negative, or smaller positive) the number, the more likely that team is expected to win. For example, a moneyline of "+110" is a more likely winner than a moneyline of "+245."

Over/Under

Vegas also sets a total-points line called the Over/Under. This is a bet on whether the combined score of both teams will land over or under that number. Example: if the O/U for a game is 45 points, betting the over means you believe the combined score will be more than 45. Betting the under means you believe it'll be less than 45.

Like the spread, this can Push if the combined score lands exactly on the line — so Vegas often uses half points here too. Example: O/U 51.5. Betting the over means you believe the combined score is 52 or more. Betting the under means you believe it's 51 or fewer.
